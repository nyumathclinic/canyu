Notes
-----


.. [#ws] The first week is a good time to do something interactive like a worksheet.  This
   would fill extra time in the case of TR classes

.. [#wk1] Monday classes do not meet this week.  So MW sections will
   only meet Wednesday.  TR sections can do more from Section 1.2
   about how changes in the function are shown in the graph.

.. [#ftc] This section covers the second Fundamental theorem of Calculus.  We review it
   because it's critical to Calc II.  The first FTC, not used in Calc II, will not be
   covered.

.. [#trig] This section is a double-length section.

.. [#int] There should be enough time left here to go over some strategies for integration
   in general.  That is, what do you do when faced with an integral and you don't know
   what to do?

.. [#vol] This section includes both "slicing" and "the disc or washer method."

.. [#de] We'll do this week out of order because differential equations need a full week.

.. [#wk7] Monday and Tuesday are holidays (Colubmus Day) here so each
   section will only meet once.  Some big sections won't have
   recitation either.  Still it's a good week for the midterm.



.. [#wk13] This is Thanksgiving week.  Wednesday is declared a Monday
   (why it's not declared a Friday I don't understand).  This doesn't
   have too much effect on MW sections, but we should be aware that
   attendance will be light on Wednesday.  TR classes will officially
   only meet once this week.
