List of Homework Assignments
----------------------------

This is a week-by-week calendar of homework assigned in Calculus II.

Dates listed are Mondays.  If you are in a TR section, your assignments are due the day
*following* the day listed.

Practice problems are neither collected nor graded, but they do cover material that may be
tested.  Answers to these and all odd-numbered problems are in the back of the textbook.
Each week three of the problems listed in the "Graded" column (indicated `like this`) will
be chosen *at random* and graded.
