Grades
------

Your course score will be determined as the following weighted average: 
 
+-------------------+--------+
| Item              | Weight |
+===================+========+
| Midterm           | 25%    |
+-------------------+--------+
| WebAssign         | 10%    |
+-------------------+--------+
| Written Homework  | 10%    |
+-------------------+--------+
| Quizzes           | 15%    |
+-------------------+--------+
| Final             | 40%    |
+-------------------+--------+
| **Total**         | 100%   |
+-------------------+--------+

We will convert this score to a letter grade beginning with these
values as cutoffs:

+---------+--------------+
| Cutoff  | Letter Grade |
+=========+==============+
| 93      | A            |
+---------+--------------+
| 90      | A-           |
+---------+--------------+
| 87      | B+           |
+---------+--------------+
| 83      | B            |
+---------+--------------+
| 80      | B-           |
+---------+--------------+
| 75      | C+           |
+---------+--------------+
| 65      | C            |
+---------+--------------+
| 50      | D            |
+---------+--------------+

These cutoffs might be adjusted, but only in the downward direction
(to make letter grades higher).
