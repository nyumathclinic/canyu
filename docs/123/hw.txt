List of Homework Assignments
----------------------------

This is a week-by-week calendar of homework assigned in |coursename|.

Dates listed are Wednesdays.  If you are in a TR or TRF section, your assignments are due the
day *following* the day listed.

Practice problems are neither collected nor graded, but they do cover material that may be
tested.  Answers to these and all odd-numbered problems are in the back of the textbook.
Each week *four* of the problems listed in the "Graded" column will be chosen *at random*
and graded.
