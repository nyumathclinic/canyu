Homework Assignments for Calculus I, Spring 2009
!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!

:Author: Matthew Leingang <leingang@cims.nyu.edu>
:Date: |date|

This is a week-by-week calendar of homework assigned in Calculus I.  

Dates listed are *Wednesdays*.  If you are in a TR section, your assignments are due the day
*following* the day listed.

Practice problems are neither collected nor graded, but they do cover material that may be
tested.  Answers to these and all odd-numbered problems are in the back of the textbook.
Each week *four* of the problems listed in the "Graded" column will
be chosen *at random* and graded.
