Notes
-----


.. [#bcp] The bottle calibration problem is a good worksheet to do at
   the beginning of the course.  

.. [#wk1] Monday classes do not meet this week.  So MW sections will
   only meet Wednesday.  TR sections can do more from Section 1.2
   about how changes in the function are shown in the graph.

.. [#wk2] as it stands this is a tough week.  It includes the squeeze 
   theorem and the intermediate value theorem.

.. [#inf] This topic should take one full class period.  The two parts
   (limits at infinity and infinite limits) are both significant and
   different.

.. [#bdr] This is the power rule and the "derivative is linear" rules

.. [#mt] This might be a good week for the midterm.

.. [#rr] I'm ambivalent about related rates problems since they seem
   to exist only for their own self-justification.  But they do help
   students develop their modeling skills, which helps with
   optimization problems.

.. [#exp] exponential and logarithmic functions are things students
   "should" know from precalculus, but I don't think they do very
   well.  So it's worth the time to make sure of it.

.. [#wk7] Monday and Tuesday are holidays (Colubmus Day) here so each
   section will only meet once.  Some big sections won't have
   recitation either.

.. [#lh] Not everybody likes to teach l'Hopital.  But I do.

.. [#wk13] This is Thanksgiving week.  Wednesday is declared a Monday
   (why it's not declared a Friday I don't understand).  This doesn't
   have too much effect on MW sections, but we should be aware that
   attendance will be light on Wednesday.  TR classes will officially
   only meet once this week.
